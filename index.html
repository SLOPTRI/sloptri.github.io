Early Detection: Mental Health ML

Multiclass Classification
4,500 records · 16 features · Kaggle Test set: 736 Beneficial vs 33 Negative

Multiclass model (Negative / Neutral / Beneficial) designed as an early preventive screening tool for school counselors and universities to assess the impact of social media habits on student well-being and academic performance.

"Recall is prioritized because, in psychological prevention, the costliest error is a false negative: failing to detect an at-risk student."
Model Macro F1 Recall (Neg) Status
Random Forest 81% 72.7% Baseline
Logistic Regression 84% 87.9% ✓ Chosen model
  • Data-leakage prevention: scikit-learn Pipeline and ColumnTransformer encapsulating median imputation, MinMaxScaler, OrdinalEncoder, and OneHotEncoder strictly on training splits.
  • Handling severe imbalance: hyperparameter tuning via GridSearchCV scoring on f1_macro combined with class_weight='balanced'.
  • Outlier strategy: critical profiles (severe insomnia, heavy depressive indicators) were intentionally preserved as authentic high-risk cases rather than removed as measurement noise.
Methodology & next steps

Feature engineering: synthesized compound risk factors like Sleep_Score = sleep duration × sleep quality to capture compounded physiological fatigue.

Roadmap: evaluate synthetic oversampling (SMOTE) on minority risk classes and cross-validate against longitudinal real-world student datasets.

SCalc

Main project · DAM Final Degree Project

Delivery couriers deal with split pay models (hourly wage plus per-order pay) and frequently track earnings in spreadsheets or notes, leading to common miscalculations. SCalc automates monthly pay tracking through customizable pay rates, daily shift logging, and productivity metrics (orders per hour).

Built offline-first with monthly "Tickets" that freeze calculated pay as immutable historical records. Architected around native Android SQLite, a clean DAO pattern, and custom POJOs.
